# Treating Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) in Children - Research Response

This response was saved from the web-research agent for review. Medical information should be verified with authoritative clinical sources and discussed with a qualified clinician.

## Summary

Evidence-based support for autistic children is individualised. Common components include behavioural supports, speech-language therapy, occupational therapy, educational accommodations, caregiver coaching, and care for co-occurring needs such as sleep difficulties, anxiety, ADHD, or seizures.

Medication may be considered for particular co-occurring symptoms under clinical supervision; it does not treat core autistic characteristics.
