## Assistant

**Evidence searched on 2026-10-06** (PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA drug labels, web)

**Bottom line**
Exercise interventions (e.g., dance, occupational therapy) show promise for improving motor skills in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), with dose-response effects reported for overall and gross motor skills (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Most important recent finding**
Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response relationships identified for overall and gross motor skills (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Immediate context**
While motor skill interventions are evidence-based, no retrieved studies directly address social communication, emotion regulation, or behavioral symptoms in ASD. Protocols for music therapy and occupational therapy-based interventions are ongoing but have not yet reported efficacy results.

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Exercise**: A dose-response meta-analysis showed significant improvements in **overall, gross, and fine motor skills** in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), with stronger effects at higher exercise doses (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A randomized controlled trial (RCT) found that a **modified ketogenic diet combined with rehabilitation training** led to greater short-term reductions in **ABC and CARS scores** (measures of behavioral and autism severity) compared to rehabilitation alone (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]).
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A systematic review identified **39 studies** (2002–2026) supporting behavioral sleep interventions for autistic children under 18, though no single intervention was universally effective (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]).
- **Caregiver-assisted NDBI**: A pilot RCT demonstrated **feasibility and preliminary efficacy** of a caregiver-assisted group-based Naturalistic Developmental Behavioral Intervention (NDBI) for autistic preschoolers, showing improvements in social and developmental outcomes (Seng et al., *Autism: International Journal of Research and Practice*, 2026 [PMID 42494078]).

### Current evidence
**Established**
- **No evidence retrieved** for guideline-endorsed or high-quality, consistent treatments for autism in children.

**Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Exercise**: Systematic review and meta-analysis (2026) established **dose-response (exploratory) improvements in motor skills** (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: Systematic review (2026) reported **heterogeneous but overall supportive evidence** for behavioral approaches to sleep problems (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]).

**Emerging**
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: RCT (2026) showed **short-term reductions in autism severity scores** (ABC, CARS) with diet + rehabilitation, but feasibility was limited to 2 months (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]).
- **Caregiver-assisted NDBI**: Pilot RCT (2026) demonstrated **promising accessibility and social developmental gains**, though one-on-one interventions remained more effective for nonverbal skills (Seng et al., *Autism: International Journal of Research and Practice*, 2026 [PMID 42494078]).

**Experimental or preliminary**
- **Music therapy**: Protocol for a systematic review/meta-analysis (2026) aims to explore **dose-response effects on social communication and emotion regulation**, but results are pending (Chen et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42486527]).
- **Occupational therapy (water competency)**: Protocol for a hybrid effectiveness-implementation trial (2026) investigates drowning prevention via occupational therapy, but efficacy is untested (Kemp et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42552003]).
- **Emotion regulation interventions**: Meta-analysis (2024) showed **significant improvements in emotion regulation/dysregulation** via non-pharmacological methods, but no specific interventions were isolated (Restoy et al., *Clinical Psychology Review*, 2024 [PMID 38401510]).

**Indirect evidence**
- **Omega-3 + inositol vs. NAC**: RCT (2026) tested these nutraceuticals for **depression/mania in emotionally dysregulated youth (with/without autism traits)**, showing trends toward benefit but **not specific to ASD** (Wozniak et al., *Journal of Affective Disorders*, 2026 [PMID 42462928]).
- **Genetic/environmental overlap**: Twin study/meta-analysis (2026) linked autistic traits to **anxiety/depression**, but **no causal treatment implications** (Han et al., *Journal of Affective Disorders*, 2026 [PMID 42508582]). *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*

**Protocol – no results**
- **Dance program (AllPlay Dance)**: RCT protocol (2025) for a community-based dance intervention targeting motor skills, but results pending (Chadha et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42711081]).

**Insufficient evidence**
- **Feeding difficulties (FEEDSETUP)**: Review (2026) proposed a structured approach but **no direct evidence for ASD-specific feeding interventions** (Fisberg et al., *Jornal de Pediatria*, 2026 [PMID 42716484]). *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*

### Key studies

1. **Wang et al. (2026)**, Meta-analysis: Exercise significantly improved **overall, gross, and fine motor skills** in children with ASD, with dose-response (exploratory) effects (PMID 42758334).
2. **Liu et al. (2026)**, Randomized controlled trial: Modified ketogenic diet + rehabilitation reduced **ABC and CARS scores** more than rehabilitation alone (PMID 42763398).
3. **Seng et al. (2026)**, Randomized controlled trial: Caregiver-assisted group-based NDBI showed **feasibility and social developmental gains** in preschoolers (PMID 42494078).
4. **Mazurek et al. (2026)**, Systematic review: **39 studies** supported behavioral sleep interventions for autistic children (PMID 42685447).
5. **Restoy et al. (2024)**, Meta-analysis: Non-pharmacological interventions improved **emotion regulation/dysregulation** in ASD (PMID 38401510).
