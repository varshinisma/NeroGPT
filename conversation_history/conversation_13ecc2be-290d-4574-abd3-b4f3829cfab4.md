## Deep research

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Exercise**: A dose-response meta-analysis showed significant improvements in **overall, gross, and fine motor skills** in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), with stronger effects at higher exercise doses (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A randomized controlled trial (RCT) found that a **modified ketogenic diet combined with rehabilitation** reduced ABC and CARS scores more than rehabilitation alone after 2 months (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]).
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A systematic review identified **39 studies** (2002–2026) supporting behavioral approaches to sleep problems in autistic children (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]).
- **Deep brain stimulation (DBS)**: A 12-month case study reported **sustained reductions in core ASD symptoms** (e.g., ATEC scores decreased by 81.3%) with bilateral dual-target DBS (Shi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42759099]).

---

### Current evidence
**Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Behavioral interventions**: Systematic reviews confirm the efficacy of **naturalistic developmental behavioral interventions (NDBIs)** for preschoolers, though group-based caregiver-assisted models show **promising but less consistent effects** than one-on-one therapy (Seng et al., *Autism*, 2026 [PMID 42494078]).

- **Exercise**: dose-response (exploratory) improvements in motor skills are **consistently reported**, but long-term functional outcomes remain understudied (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Sleep interventions**: Behavioral strategies (e.g., bedtime routines) are **widely supported**, but individual responses vary (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]).

**Emerging**
- **Extended reality (XR)**: Virtual/augmented reality shows **growing research interest**, particularly for training and therapy, though no preliminary efficacy data yet (Hao et al., *International Journal of Medical Informatics*, 2026 [PMID 42784985]).
- **Dietary interventions**: The ketogenic diet’s **short-term benefits** (2 months) are preliminary; long-term data and mechanisms are lacking (Liu et al., 2026 [PMID 42763398]).

**Experimental or preliminary**
- **Neurophysiological markers**: Sleep spindle abnormalities are **associated with ASD**, but their role as biomarkers or therapeutic targets is speculative (Erçelebi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42753649]).

**Indirect evidence**
- **Feeding difficulties**: A structured algorithm (*FEEDSETUP*) addresses pediatric feeding issues broadly, but its applicability to ASD-specific needs is **not established** (Fisberg et al., *Jornal de Pediatria*, 2026 [PMID 42716484]). *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*

**Protocol – no results**
- **Dance programs** (AllPlay Dance), **occupational therapy in water** (AquOTic), and **music therapy** are under investigation but **do not yet establish efficacy** (Chadha et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42711081]; Kemp et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42552003]; Chen et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42486527]).

### What remains under investigation
- **Efficacy**: Head-to-head trials comparing exercise, diet, or DBS to standard behavioral therapies are **not yet available**.
- **Effectiveness**: Real-world implementation of XR, dance programs, or occupational therapy in water is **unassessed**.
- **Feasibility**: Long-term adherence to ketogenic diets or DBS is **not established**.
- **Association**: Neurophysiological markers (e.g., sleep spindles) are **correlational**; causality is speculative (Erçelebi et al., 2026 [PMID 42753649]).
- **Gaps**: No trials address **social communication** (Chen et al., *protocol*, 2026 [PMID 42486527]) or **gait/rhythm interventions** (NCT06659328, *completed but no results*).
