**Bottom line**
Exercise interventions, particularly structured programs like dance or occupational therapy, show evidence of improving motor skills in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), with dose-response effects reported in recent meta-analyses (Wang et al., *European journal of pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Most important recent finding**
Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response relationships identified for overall and gross motor skills (Wang et al., *European journal of pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Immediate context**
While motor skill interventions are promising, ongoing trials and protocols (e.g., *AllPlay Dance*, *AquOTic*) explore broader functional outcomes, but no direct evidence yet addresses social communication or emotional regulation in ASD.

## Deep research

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Exercise interventions**: A dose-response meta-analysis (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]) showed significant improvements in **overall, gross, and fine motor skills** in children with ASD, with clear dose-response effects for overall and gross motor skills.
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A randomized controlled trial (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]) found **greater short-term reductions in ABC and CARS scores** (measures of behavioral and autism severity) when combined with rehabilitation, compared to rehabilitation alone.
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A systematic review (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]) identified **39 studies** (2002–2026) supporting behavioral approaches for sleep problems in autistic children.
- **Extended reality (XR)**: A review (Hao et al., *International Journal of Medical Informatics*, 2026 [PMID 42784985]) highlighted **virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR)** as promising modalities for autism support, though no efficacy data were reported.
- **Deep brain stimulation (DBS)**: A 12-month case study (Shi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42759099]) reported **sustained reductions in core ASD symptoms** (e.g., ATEC, RBS-R scores) with bilateral dual-target DBS, though this was a single-case observation.

### Current evidence
**Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: Systematic review (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]) reports behavioral strategies improve sleep outcomes in autistic children (39 studies, 2002–2026).

- **Exercise**: Meta-analysis (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]) demonstrates **motor skill improvements** but lacks long-term or head-to-head comparisons.
- **Naturalistic Developmental Behavioral Interventions (NDBIs)**: Pilot RCT (Seng et al., *Autism*, 2026 [PMID 42494078]) shows **feasibility and preliminary efficacy** for caregiver-assisted group-based interventions, though one-on-one effects were stronger.

**Emerging**
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: RCT (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]) suggests **short-term behavioral benefits** when paired with rehabilitation, but follow-up is limited.
- **Extended reality (XR)**: Review (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]) identifies **growing interest** in VR/AR for autism support, but no efficacy data were synthesized.

**Experimental or preliminary**
- **Sleep spindle biomarkers**: Observational study (Erçelebi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42753649]) links **spindle characteristics** to ASD, but causality is unexplored.

**Indirect evidence**
- **Oral care interventions**: Systematic review (Floríndez et al., *Autism*, 2025 [PMID 39056277]) addresses **dental challenges** in autistic individuals but does not evaluate ASD-specific outcomes.

**Protocol – no results**
- **Dance program (AllPlay Dance)**: Protocol (Chadha et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42711081]) investigates community-based dance for motor skills but has not yet reported outcomes.
- **Music therapy dosage**: Protocol (Chen et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42486527]) plans a meta-analysis of music therapy’s effect on **social communication** but lacks raw data.
- **Occupational therapy (AquOTic)**: Protocol (Kemp et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42552003]) tests water competency training but results are pending.

**Insufficient evidence**
