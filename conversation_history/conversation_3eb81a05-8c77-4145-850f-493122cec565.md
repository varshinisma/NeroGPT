Exercise interventions show a dose-response benefit for motor skills in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), with improvements in overall, gross, and fine motor function (Wang et al., *European journal of pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Most important recent finding**
Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response relationships identified for overall and gross motor skills (Wang et al., *European journal of pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Immediate context**
Motor impairments are a key barrier to functional independence in ASD, and structured interventions like dance or occupational therapy are being explored for broader developmental outcomes. No direct evidence yet supports social communication or emotion regulation improvements from music therapy.

## Deep research

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Exercise**: A **multilevel meta-analysis** (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]) found supported by limited evidence dose-response improvements in **gross and fine motor skills** in children with ASD, with significant effects on overall motor function.
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A **randomized controlled trial** (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]) reported **short-term reductions in ABC and CARS scores** (assessing behavior and autism severity) when combined with rehabilitation, compared to rehabilitation alone. **Feasibility reported**; results not yet replicated long-term.
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A **systematic review** (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]) summarized **39 studies** (2002–2026) on behavioral sleep interventions in autistic children, though **no direct efficacy comparison** was performed.
- **Caregiver-assisted NDBI**: A **pilot RCT** (Seng et al., *Autism*, 2026 [PMID 42494078]) showed **promising but limited efficacy** for group-based naturalistic interventions, with **nonverbal developmental gains only in one-on-one settings**.

### Current evidence
**Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Exercise**: Systematic review and meta-analysis (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]) reports **consistent improvement in motor skills** across studies, with dose-response relationships established.

- **Modified ketogenic diet**: RCT (Liu et al., 2026 [PMID 42763398]) demonstrates **short-term behavioral improvements** (ABC/CARS scores) but lacks long-term or head-to-head data.
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: Systematic review (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]) highlights **widespread use** but does not establish superiority over other methods.

**Emerging**
- **Caregiver-assisted NDBI**: Pilot RCT (Seng et al., 2026 [PMID 42494078]) suggests **accessible early intervention benefits**, though efficacy varies by delivery format.
- **Omega-3 + inositol/NAC**: RCT (Wozniak et al., 2026 [PMID 42462928]) reports **trends toward mood/depression improvement in emotionally dysregulated youth with ASD traits** (indirect population; not ASD-specific).

**Experimental or preliminary**
- **Music therapy dosage**: Protocol-only (Chen et al., 2026 [PMID 42486527]) awaits meta-analysis results for **social communication/emotion regulation**.
- **Occupational therapy (water competency)**: Protocol (Kemp et al., 2026 [PMID 42552003]) investigates **drowning prevention** via water skills; no efficacy data yet.
- **Dance program (AllPlay Dance)**: Protocol (Chadha et al., 2026 [PMID 42711081]) targets **motor skills via community-based dance**; results pending.

**Indirect evidence**
- **Feeding difficulties (FEEDSETUP)**: Review (Fisberg et al., 2026 [PMID 42716484]) proposes a **structured feeding approach** for pediatric difficulties (different population; not ASD-specific).
- **Genetic/environmental overlap**: Meta-analysis/twin study (Han et al., 2026 [PMID 42508582]) links ASD traits to **anxiety/depression comorbidity** (not treatment-focused). *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*

**Protocol – no results**
- **Lactobacillus PS128**: Terminated trial (NCT05307744) due to COVID-19; **no efficacy data** for probiotic intervention in ASD.

**Insufficient evidence**
- **Prenatal antiseizure meds**: Case-control (Hernández-Díaz et al., 2024 [PMID 38507750]) reports **higher ASD risk with valproate/topiramate exposure** (risk factor, not treatment).

- **Efficacy**: Head-to-head comparisons of exercise vs. behavioral interventions for motor/social outcomes.
