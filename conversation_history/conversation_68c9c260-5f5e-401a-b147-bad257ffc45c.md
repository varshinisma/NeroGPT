How to treat Autism in childrens?

---

## Assistant

### **Bottom line**
There is **no FDA-approved pharmacologic treatment for core autism spectrum disorder (ASD) symptoms** (social communication deficits or restricted/repetitive behaviors). **Behavioral interventions** (e.g., applied behavior analysis) remain the **gold-standard non-pharmacologic approach**, supported by systematic reviews. **Off-label medications** (e.g., risperidone, aripiprazole) may target comorbid symptoms (e.g., irritability, aggression), but evidence is limited to specific subpopulations. **Leucovorin** is FDA-approved **only for cerebral folate deficiency (CFD)**, not ASD itself, despite prior misinformation.

### **Latest Findings (Deep Answer)**
1. **Behavioral interventions remain the cornerstone for ASD management.**
   - **Mazurek et al. (2026)** [PMID 42685447] confirmed that **sleep problems in autistic children respond best to behavioral strategies**, with no pharmacologic superiority reported.
   - **Sandbank et al. (2023)** [PMID 37963634] (meta-analysis) supported **early childhood behavioral interventions** (e.g., applied behavior analysis) as the most evidence-backed approach for core ASD symptoms.

2. **No pharmacologic treatment is FDA-approved for core ASD.**
   - **Web sources (2025–2026)** clarified that **leucovorin’s FDA approval is limited to CFD**, not ASD. Prior claims of autism treatment were **misleading** (FDA, 2026).
   - **Panda et al. (2024)** [PMID 39243316] (retracted RCT) suggested folinic acid *might* benefit ASD, but **retraction invalidates this claim**.

3. **Off-label medications for comorbid symptoms:**
   - **Risperidone/aripiprazole** (for irritability/aggression) showed **mixed weight gain risks** (NCT04903353, 2026).
   - **Buspirone/mirtazapine** are **guideline-preferred** for anxiety in ASD (Springer, 2025).

### **Conflicting Evidence**
1. **Retracted Study on Folinic Acid (2024):**
   - **Panda et al. (2024)** [PMID 39243316] initially suggested folinic acid may benefit ASD, but the study was **retracted** due to **methodological concerns**. No valid evidence supports its use for ASD.
   - **Implication:** Avoid relying on this retracted data for clinical decisions.

2. **FDA vs. Media Misinterpretation:**
   - **Web sources (2026)** clarified that **leucovorin’s FDA approval is for CFD only**, not ASD. Prior media reports (e.g., *Disability Scoop*) **overstated its use for autism**.
   - **Implication:** **Do not prescribe leucovorin for ASD unless CFD is confirmed.**

---
### **What Remains Under Investigation**
1. **Neurobiological Targets:**
   - **Oxytocin analogs** (e.g., intranasal oxytocin) are being studied for social deficits but **no Phase 3 trials yet**.
   - **GABAergic modulators** (e.g., baclofen) show **preliminary promise** for repetitive behaviors but lack robust RCT data.

2. **Behavioral Augmentation:**
   - **Digital therapeutics** (e.g., AI-driven social skills training) are emerging but **no large-scale efficacy trials published yet**.

3. **Comorbid Conditions:**
   - **Epilepsy/ADHD in ASD:** Trials for **levetiracetam** and **methylphenidate** are ongoing but **not specific to ASD core symptoms**.

---
### **Evidence Limitations**
1. **No FDA-approved pharmacologic treatment for core ASD exists.**
   - All pharmacologic options are **off-label** or investigational.

2. **Retracted study on folinic acid (2024) invalidates prior claims.**
   - **Panda et al. (2024)** was retracted; **do not use this data for clinical decisions**.

3. **Trial registry limitations:**
   - **NCT05307744** was terminated due to **COVID-19-related recruitment challenges**; results may not generalize.
   - **No active Phase 3 trials** for core ASD symptoms (social communication/repetitive behaviors).

4. **Web sources lack peer review:**
   - FDA clarifications (2026) are **authoritative**, but **media reports (e.g., Disability Scoop) may mislead** without context.
