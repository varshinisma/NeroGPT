## Assistant

**Evidence searched on 2026-10-07** (PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA drug labels, web)

**Bottom line**
Exercise interventions, particularly structured programs like dance or occupational therapy, show promise in improving motor skills in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), with dose-response effects reported for overall and gross motor skills (Wang et al., *European journal of pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Most important recent finding**
Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response relationships identified for overall and gross motor skills (Wang et al., *European journal of pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Immediate context**
While motor skill interventions are evidence-based, targeted treatments for core ASD symptoms (e.g., social communication) are still under active investigation, with protocols for music therapy and occupational-based water competency interventions currently ongoing or planned. No direct efficacy data for these interventions have been published yet.

## Deep research

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A randomized controlled trial (RCT) reported **short-term reductions** in ABC and CARS scores (measures of behavioral and autism severity) in children with ASD after 2 months, with the diet combined with rehabilitation outperforming rehabilitation alone (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]). **Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A systematic review identified **39 studies** (2002–2026) supporting behavioral approaches to sleep problems in autistic children, though no single intervention was universally effective (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]). **Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Deep brain stimulation (DBS)**: A **12-month case study** demonstrated sustained reductions in core ASD symptoms (e.g., ATEC, RBS-R scores) with bilateral dual-target DBS in a single pediatric patient (Shi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42759099]). **Experimental or preliminary**

### Current evidence
#### **Established**
- **No established treatments**: No guideline-endorsed or consistently high-quality evidence supports a single intervention for core ASD symptoms (e.g., social communication, restricted/repetitive behaviors) in children. **Insufficient evidence**

#### **Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Naturalistic Developmental Behavioral Interventions (NDBIs)**: A pilot RCT found **caregiver-assisted group-based NDBI** improved developmental and social outcomes in preschoolers, though one-on-one interventions showed greater gains in nonverbal skills (Seng et al., *Autism*, 2026 [PMID 42494078]).
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: Systematic review reports efficacy for sleep problems, but **no standardized protocol** emerged (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]).
- **Exercise**: Meta-analysis supports **motor skill improvements** with dose-response (exploratory) effects, but **no data on social/behavioral outcomes** (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

#### **Emerging**
- **Extended Reality (XR)**: A systematic review highlighted **61 VR/AR studies** (2020–2026) showing promise for training, education, and therapy, but **no efficacy data** for core ASD symptoms (Hao et al., *International Journal of Medical Informatics*, 2026 [PMID 42784985]).
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: RCT suggests **short-term behavioral benefits**, but follow-up >2 months and long-term effects **not reported** (Liu et al., 2026 [PMID 42763398]).

#### **Experimental or preliminary**
- **Deep brain stimulation (DBS)**: Single-case study reports **sustained symptom reduction** (12 months), but **no RCT data** (Shi et al., 2026 [PMID 42759099]).
- **Sleep spindle characteristics**: Observational study identified **neurophysiological markers** (reduced spindle activity) linked to ASD, but **no therapeutic implication** (Erçelebi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42753649]).

#### **Indirect evidence**
- **Feeding difficulties (FEEDSETUP)**: A review proposed a **structured approach** for pediatric feeding issues (a related but distinct population), not ASD core symptoms (Fisberg et al., *Jornal de Pediatria*, 2026 [PMID 42716484]). *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*

### Non-drug treatments
- **Exercise**: supported by limited evidence for motor skills (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: **Evidence-supported but limited** (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]).
- **NDBIs**: **Evidence-supported but limited** (Seng et al., 2026 [PMID 42494078]).
- **XR interventions**: **Emerging** (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]).
- **Nature-based activities**: Qualitative study suggests **parent-reported benefits** (e.g., communication, calm) but **no quantitative efficacy data** (Galbraith et al., 2026 [PMID 42603141]).

### Key studies

1. **Wang et al. (2026)**, meta-analysis: “Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD.” (PMID 42758334)
2. **Hao et al. (2026)**, systematic review: “There was a significant increase in XR autism research after 2020, with virtual reality (VR) as the largest modality (n = 61, 53.0%), followed by augmented reality (AR) (n = 36, 31.3%).” (PMID 42784985)
3. **Mazurek et al. (2026)**, systematic review: “Most studies (95%) found that behavioral interventions were effective for at least some sleep outcomes.” (PMID 42685447)
4. **Sandbank et al. (2023)**, meta-analysis: “When effects were then restricted to exclude those at high risk of detection bias, only one significant summary effect was estimated-naturalistic developmental behavioral interventions on measures of diagnostic characteristics of autism (0.30, 0.03 to 0.57; P=0.03).” (PMID 37963634)
5. **Zeidan et al. (2022)**, systematic review: “Prevalence estimates increased over time and varied greatly within and across sociodemographic groups.” (PMID 35238171)

### What remains under investigation
- **Efficacy**: Head-to-head trials comparing **NDBIs vs. traditional therapy**, **XR vs. conventional interventions**, or **long-term ketogenic diet effects**.
- **Effectiveness**: Real-world implementation of **behavioral sleep interventions** and **exercise programs** in diverse populations.
- **Feasibility**: Scalability of **DBS** and **caregiver-assisted group NDBIs** in resource-limited settings.
- **Association**: Longitudinal studies linking **sleep spindle characteristics** (Erçelebi et al., 2026 [PMID 42753649]) to **prognosis or treatment response**.
- **Protocol**: **NCT06659328** (completed, results pending) tests structured walking interventions for gait parameters in ASD.
