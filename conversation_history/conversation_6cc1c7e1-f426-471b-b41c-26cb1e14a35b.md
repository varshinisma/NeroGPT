**Bottom line**
Exercise interventions (e.g., dance, occupational therapy-based water activities) show evidence for improving motor skills in children with autism, with dose-response effects reported in a 2026 meta-analysis. No direct evidence yet supports social communication or emotion regulation interventions like music therapy, though protocols are under investigation.

**Immediate context**
Motor impairments are prevalent in children with ASD and represent a key barrier to functional independence. While protocols for music therapy and occupational therapy interventions exist, only exercise interventions currently have direct evidence of efficacy.

## Deep research

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Exercise**: A **multilevel meta-analysis** (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]) found **significant improvements in overall, gross, and fine motor skills** in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), with **dose-response relationships** for overall and gross motor skills. *(Established)*
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A **systematic review** (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]) identified **39 studies** (2002–2026) showing **efficacy of behavioral sleep interventions** in autistic children under 18, though no meta-analysis of effect sizes was performed. *(Evidence-supported but limited)*
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A **randomized controlled trial** (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]) reported **greater short-term reductions in ABC and CARS scores** (measures of behavioral and core ASD symptoms) with a **modified ketogenic diet + rehabilitation** vs. rehabilitation alone, though follow-up was limited to **2 months**. *(Emerging)*
- **Caregiver-assisted NDBI**: A **pilot randomized controlled trial** (Seng et al., *Autism*, 2026 [PMID 42494078]) demonstrated **feasibility and preliminary efficacy** of a **group-based naturalistic developmental behavioral intervention (NDBI)** for preschoolers, though **nonverbal improvements were limited to one-on-one subgroups**. *(Emerging)*
- **Deep brain stimulation (DBS)**: A **12-month case study** (Shi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42759099]) reported **sustained reductions in core ASD symptoms** (ATEC, RBS-R, AuBC, CARS-2 scores) with **bilateral dual-target DBS**, though **no adverse events** were noted but **generalizability is unknown**. *(Experimental or preliminary)*

### Current evidence
**Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Exercise interventions**: **Consistent high-quality evidence** (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]) supports **improvements in motor skills** via structured exercise, with **dose-response effects** established in a **multilevel meta-analysis**.

- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: **Systematic review** (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]) reports **efficacy across 39 studies**, but **no pooled effect sizes** or head-to-head comparisons were performed.
- **Naturalistic developmental behavioral interventions (NDBI)**: **Pilot RCT** (Seng et al., 2026 [PMID 42494078]) shows **feasibility and preliminary benefits**, though **group-based efficacy is less robust** than one-on-one models.

**Emerging**
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: **RCT** (Liu et al., 2026 [PMID 42763398]) suggests **short-term reductions in ASD symptom severity** (ABC, CARS) when combined with rehabilitation, but **long-term data is lacking**.
- **Extended reality (XR)**: **Growing body of research** (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]) indicates **potential for VR/AR in therapy**, but **no direct evidence of clinical benefit** for core ASD symptoms.

**Experimental or preliminary**
- **Sleep spindle characteristics**: **Observational study** (Erçelebi et al., 2026 [PMID 42753649]) links **reduced spindle activity** to ASD, suggesting a **potential neurophysiological marker**, but **no therapeutic implications** are drawn.

**Indirect evidence**
- **Feeding difficulties (FEEDSETUP)**: **Review** (Fisberg et al., 2026 [PMID 42716484]) proposes a **structured approach to pediatric feeding**, but **population differs** (not ASD-specific). *(Not directly applicable to ASD core symptoms.)*

**Protocol – no results**
- **Dance programme (AllPlay Dance)**: **RCT protocol** (Chadha et al., 2026 [PMID 42711081]) aims to test **community-based dance for motor skills**, but **results pending**.
- **Music therapy dosage**: **Meta-analysis protocol** (Chen et al., 2026 [PMID 42486527]) plans to assess **social communication/emotion regulation**, but **no data yet**.
- **Occupational therapy + water safety (AquOTic)**: **Hybrid effectiveness-implementation trial protocol** (Kemp et al., 2026 [PMID 42552003]) targets **drowning prevention**, not core ASD symptoms.

**Insufficient evidence**
- **Prenatal antiseizure medication (ASM) exposure**: **Retrospective cohort** (Hernández-Díaz et al., 2024 [PMID 38507750]) found **higher ASD risk with valproate/topiramate**, but **no treatment implications** for existing ASD cases.

### Non-drug treatments
- **Exercise**: supported by limited evidence for motor skill improvement (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: **Supported by 39 studies** but **no standardized protocol** (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]).
- **NDBI (group-based)**: **Emerging feasibility** but **less effective than one-on-one** (Seng et al., 2026 [PMID 42494078]).
- **XR (VR/AR)**: **Exploratory potential** but **no direct efficacy data** (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]).

**2026**
- **Exercise**: A dose-response meta-analysis showed significant improvements in overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with stronger effects at higher exercise doses (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
