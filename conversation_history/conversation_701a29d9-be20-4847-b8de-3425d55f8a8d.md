**Most important recent finding**
Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response relationships identified for overall and gross motor skills. (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334])

## Deep research

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Exercise**: A **meta-analysis** (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]) found supported by limited evidence dose-response improvements in **motor skills** (overall, gross, and fine) for children with autism, with higher doses linked to greater gains.
- **Behavioral interventions**: A **systematic review** (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]) reported **evidence-supported but limited** efficacy of behavioral sleep interventions for autistic children, though no specific dose or protocol was endorsed.
- **Extended reality (XR)**: A **systematic review** (Hao et al., *International Journal of Medical Informatics*, 2026 [PMID 42784985]) highlighted **emerging** potential for VR/AR in autism support, but no direct efficacy data was synthesized.
- **Deep brain stimulation (DBS)**: A **case study** (Shi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42759099]) reported **experimental or preliminary** sustained reductions in core autism symptoms (e.g., ATEC scores dropped 81.3% at 12 months), though limited to one patient.
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A **randomized controlled trial** (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]) showed **emerging** short-term reductions in ABC and CARS scores (vs. rehabilitation alone), but feasibility was the primary focus.

### Current evidence
- **Evidence-supported but limited**: No guidelines or consistent high-quality evidence were retrieved for **core autism symptom management** (e.g., social communication, repetitive behaviors). **Motor skills** (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]) and **sleep interventions** (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]) are **evidence-supported but limited**.
- **Emerging**:
  - **Behavioral therapies**: Caregiver-assisted group-based **Naturalistic Developmental Behavioral Interventions (NDBIs)** (Seng et al., 2026 [PMID 42494078]) showed **preliminary efficacy** for preschoolers, though one-on-one interventions remained superior.
  - **Dietary interventions**: The **modified ketogenic diet** (Liu et al., 2026 [PMID 42763398]) demonstrated **short-term associations** with symptom reduction, but long-term data is lacking.
  - **XR technologies**: VR/AR interventions (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]) are **under investigation** for engagement and skill-building, but no direct efficacy proof.
- **Experimental or preliminary**:
  - **Sleep biomarkers**: Altered sleep spindles (Erçelebi et al., 2026 [PMID 42753649]) may serve as **neurophysiological markers**, but no treatment implications were drawn.
- **Indirect evidence**:
  - **Physical activity** (Tao et al., 2025 [PMID 39806448]) improved cognitive functions in **neurodevelopmental disorders generally**, not autism specifically. *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*
  - **Feeding difficulties** (Fisberg et al., 2026 [PMID 42716484]) addressed a **different population** (pediatric feeding disorders), not autism core symptoms. *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*
- **Insufficient evidence**: No protocols or trials established efficacy for **social communication**, **emotion regulation**, or **psychotic features** in autism.

### Non-drug treatments
- **Exercise**: supported by limited evidence for motor skills (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: **Evidence-supported but limited** (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]).
- **NDBIs (group-based)**: **Emerging** for preschoolers (Seng et al., 2026 [PMID 42494078]).
- **XR (VR/AR)**: **Under investigation** (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]).
- **Nature-based activities**: **Indirect evidence** of developmental benefits (Galbraith et al., 2026 [PMID 42603141]), but no autism-specific focus.

### Key studies

1. **Wang et al. (2026)**, Meta-Analysis: Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response relationships identified (PMID 42758334).
2. **Liu et al. (2026)**, Randomized Controlled Trial: A modified ketogenic diet combined with rehabilitation reduced ABC and CARS scores more than rehabilitation alone (PMID 42763398).
3. **Seng et al. (2026)**, Randomized Controlled Trial: Caregiver-assisted group-based NDBI improved developmental and social outcomes in preschoolers (PMID 42494078).
4. **Mazurek et al. (2026)**, Systematic Review: Behavioral sleep interventions improved sleep outcomes in autistic children (PMID 42685447).

### What remains under investigation
- **Efficacy**:
  - **Social communication/emotion regulation**: No direct evidence; music therapy protocols (Chen et al., 2026 [PMID 42486527]) are **under investigation**.
- **Effectiveness**: Behavioral sleep interventions (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]) lack **real-world effectiveness** data beyond controlled settings.
- **Feasibility**: Group-based NDBIs (Seng et al., 2026 [PMID 42494078]) are **promising but not yet supported** for scalability.
- **Association**: Sleep spindle biomarkers (Erçelebi et al., 2026 [PMID 42753649]) may correlate with ASD but do not inform treatment.
