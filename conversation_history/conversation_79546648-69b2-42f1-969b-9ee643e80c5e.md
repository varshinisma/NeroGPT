## Deep research

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Exercise**: A dose-response meta-analysis showed significant improvements in **overall, gross, and fine motor skills** in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), with stronger effects at higher exercise doses (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A systematic review of 39 studies reported that behavioral sleep interventions **improve sleep outcomes** in autistic children under 18 (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]).
- **Extended reality (XR)**: A systematic review identified **61 virtual reality (VR) and 36 augmented reality (AR) studies** supporting XR interventions for ASD, with hybrid systems and training modalities being the most common (Hao et al., *International Journal of Medical Informatics*, 2026 [PMID 42784985]).
- **Deep brain stimulation (DBS)**: A 12-month case study reported **sustained reductions in core ASD symptoms** (e.g., ATEC scores decreased by 81.3%) with bilateral dual-target DBS in a child with severe autism (Shi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42759099]).
- **Sleep spindles**: Observational findings linked **reduced spindle amplitude, number, and density** to ASD, suggesting potential neurophysiological markers (Erçelebi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42753649]).

### Current evidence
**Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: Systematic review evidence supports their efficacy for sleep problems in autistic children (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]).

- **Emotion regulation interventions**: Meta-analysis shows significant improvements in emotion regulation and dysregulation in ASD, but no specific treatment modality is highlighted (Restoy et al., 2024 [PMID 38401510]).

**Emerging**
- **Extended reality (XR)**: Growing body of research (67 studies) suggests potential for VR/AR in ASD, but no direct evidence of clinical efficacy (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]).
- **Sleep biomarkers**: Reduced sleep spindle characteristics may correlate with ASD, but causality is not established (Erçelebi et al., 2026 [PMID 42753649]).

**Experimental or preliminary**
- **Nature-based interventions**: Qualitative parent reports suggest benefits for communication and calmness, but no quantitative efficacy data (Galbraith et al., 2026 [PMID 42603141]).
- **Occupational therapy (water competency)**: Protocol under investigation; no results available (Kemp et al., 2026 [PMID 42552003]).

**Indirect evidence**
- **Feeding difficulties**: A structured algorithm (FEEDSETUP) addresses pediatric feeding challenges, but its application to ASD-specific feeding difficulties is not directly supported (Fisberg et al., 2026 [PMID 42716484]). *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*

**Protocol – no results**
- **Dance program (AllPlay Dance)**: RCT protocol for community-based dance interventions in ASD; results pending (Chadha et al., 2026 [PMID 42711081]).
- **Music therapy dosage**: Systematic review protocol for music therapy’s effect on social communication; no data yet (Chen et al., 2026 [PMID 42486527]).

**Insufficient evidence**
- **Probiotics (Lactobacillus Plantarum PS128)**: Terminated trial due to COVID-19; no published efficacy data (NCT05307744).

### Non-drug treatments
- **Exercise**: dose-response (exploratory) improvements in motor skills (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: Strong evidence for sleep outcomes (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]).
- **Extended reality (VR/AR)**: Emerging but unproven clinical efficacy (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]).
- **Nature-based activities**: Parent-reported benefits, but no direct evidence (Galbraith et al., 2026 [PMID 42603141]).

1. **Wang et al. (2026)**, Meta-analysis: Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response effects (PMID 42758334).

### What remains under investigation
- **Efficacy**: Head-to-head comparisons of exercise, XR, and behavioral interventions for motor/social outcomes.
- **Effectiveness**: Real-world implementation of DBS or high-dose exercise in routine care.
- **Feasibility**: Scalability of nature-based or dance interventions (Chadha et al., 2026 [PMID 42711081]; Galbraith et al., 2026 [PMID 42603141]).
- **Association**: Longitudinal studies linking sleep biomarkers (e.g., spindles) to ASD progression (Erçelebi et al., 2026 [PMID 42753649]).
- **Gaps**: No trials test **social communication** outcomes for exercise or XR; terminated probiotic trial (NCT05307744) leaves unanswered questions.
