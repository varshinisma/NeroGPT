## User

How to treat autism in children?

---

## Assistant

### **Bottom line**
There is **no FDA-approved pharmacologic treatment specifically for core autism symptoms** in children. Current pharmacologic approaches target **co-occurring symptoms** (e.g., irritability, hyperactivity, anxiety) with off-label medications like risperidone or aripiprazole, supported by limited evidence. Emerging investigational agents (e.g., edaravone, flurbiprofen) target oxidative stress and neuroinflammation but are **not approved** for ASD. Behavioral and psychological interventions remain the **primary evidence-based treatment**.

*Immediate context:* No pharmacologic treatment is FDA-approved for core autism symptoms, but adjunctive therapies for comorbid symptoms (e.g., irritability) are widely used off-label. Emerging mechanistic targets (e.g., oxidative stress) are under investigation but lack pediatric clinical validation.
