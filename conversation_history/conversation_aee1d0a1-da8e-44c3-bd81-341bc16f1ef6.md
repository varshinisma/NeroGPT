**Bottom line**
Exercise interventions show a dose-response effect improving motor skills in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), with evidence supporting structured programs like dance or occupational therapy (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]). Feeding difficulties require individualized, evidence-based approaches, though this evidence is indirect (Fisberg et al., *Jornal de Pediatria*, 2026 [PMID 42716484]).

**Most important recent finding**
Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response relationships identified for overall and gross motor skills (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Immediate context**
While motor skill interventions (e.g., dance, occupational therapy) are evidence-based, social communication and emotion regulation remain under investigation, with protocols for music therapy and structured walking interventions currently underway. Feeding difficulties require tailored, non-diagnosis-centric treatment models.

## Deep research

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Exercise**: A **multilevel meta-analysis** (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]) found supported by limited evidence dose-response improvements in **motor skills** (overall, gross, and fine) for children with ASD, with higher doses linked to greater gains.
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A **systematic review** (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]) identified **39 studies** (2002–2026) supporting behavioral approaches for sleep problems in autistic children, though no single intervention was universally effective.
- **Extended reality (XR)**: A **systematic review** (Hao et al., *International Journal of Medical Informatics*, 2026 [PMID 42784985]) highlighted **61 VR/36 AR studies**, predominantly hybrid or training-focused, but **no direct efficacy data** was synthesized.
- **Deep brain stimulation (DBS)**: A **12-month case study** (Shi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42759099]) reported **sustained reductions** in core ASD symptoms (e.g., ATEC scores down 81.3%) via bilateral DBS targeting the anterior capsula and ventral striatum, with **no adverse events**.

### Current evidence
**Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Motor skills**: Exercise interventions **significantly improve** gross and fine motor skills in ASD (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Behavioral interventions**: **Systematic reviews** (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]) confirm behavioral sleep strategies reduce sleep problems in autistic children, though heterogeneity exists.

#### **Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Naturalistic Developmental Behavioral Interventions (NDBIs)**: A **pilot RCT** (Seng et al., *Autism*, 2026 [PMID 42494078]) showed **feasibility and preliminary efficacy** for caregiver-assisted group-based NDBIs, though one-on-one interventions remained superior for nonverbal development.
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A **small RCT** (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]) linked the diet + rehabilitation to **greater short-term reductions** in ABC/CARS scores vs. rehabilitation alone, but **no long-term data** was reported.

#### **Emerging**
- **Extended reality (XR)**: Growing research (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]) suggests **potential** for VR/AR in ASD, but **no high-quality efficacy data** exists yet.
- **Sleep neurophysiology**: Observational findings (Erçelebi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42753649]) link **reduced sleep spindle activity** to ASD, hinting at a **biomarker** but not a treatment.

#### **Experimental or preliminary**
- **Music therapy**: A **protocol** (Chen et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42486527]) awaits meta-analysis on **dose-response effects** for social communication/emotion regulation.

#### **Indirect evidence**
- **Feeding difficulties**: A **review** (Fisberg et al., *Jornal de Pediatria*, 2026 [PMID 42716484]) proposed a **FEEDSETUP algorithm** for pediatric feeding issues, but **not specific to ASD**.

#### **Insufficient evidence**
- **Oral hygiene**: A **protocol** (NCT07859696) tests peer modeling for plaque reduction, but **no results** are available.
- **Probiotics**: A **terminated trial** (NCT05307744) failed to assess *Lactobacillus plantarum* PS128’s impact on social responsiveness due to **recruitment challenges**.

### Non-drug treatments
1. **Exercise**: supported by limited evidence for motor skills (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
2. **Behavioral sleep interventions**: **Supported** by systematic reviews (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]).
3. **NDBIs**: **Preliminary efficacy** for social/language development (Seng et al., 2026 [PMID 42494078]).
4. **XR (VR/AR)**: **Emerging** but **no direct efficacy proof** (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]).

### Key studies

1. **Wang et al. (2026)**, meta-analysis: “Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD.” (PMID 42758334)
2. **Hao et al. (2026)**, systematic review: “There was a significant increase in XR autism research after 2020, with virtual reality (VR) as the largest modality (n = 61, 53.0%), followed by augmented reality (AR) (n = 36, 31.3%).” (PMID 42784985)
3. **Mazurek et al. (2026)**, systematic review: “Most studies (95%) found that behavioral interventions were effective for at least some sleep outcomes.” (PMID 42685447)
4. **Sandbank et al. (2023)**, meta-analysis: “When effects were then restricted to exclude those at high risk of detection bias, only one significant summary effect was estimated-naturalistic developmental behavioral interventions on measures of diagnostic characteristics of autism (0.30, 0.03 to 0.57; P=0.03).” (PMID 37963634)
5. **Zeidan et al. (2022)**, systematic review: “Prevalence estimates increased over time and varied greatly within and across sociodemographic groups.” (PMID 35238171)

### What remains under investigation
- **Efficacy**: Head-to-head comparisons of **NDBIs vs. traditional therapy**, **XR modalities** (VR/AR), and **long-term DBS outcomes**.
- **Effectiveness**: Real-world adoption of **behavioral sleep interventions** and **ketogenic diet** in diverse populations.
- **Feasibility**: Scaling **group-based NDBIs** and **community dance programs** (e.g., AllPlay Dance; Chadha et al., 2026 [PMID 42711081] protocol).
- **Association**: Clarifying **sleep spindle biomarkers** (Erçelebi et al., 2026 [PMID 42753649]) and their role in ASD pathophysiology.
- **Ongoing trials**:
  - **NCT06659328** (Completed): Structured walking intervention for gait parameters (results pending).
  - **NCT07859696** (Not yet recruiting): Peer modeling for oral hygiene (no results expected soon).
