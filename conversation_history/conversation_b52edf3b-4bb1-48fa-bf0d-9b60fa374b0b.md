**Evidence searched on 2026-10-07** (PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA drug labels, web)

**Bottom line**
Exercise interventions show a dose-response effect improving motor skills in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), with significant gains in overall, gross, and fine motor function (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Most important recent finding**
Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response relationships identified for overall and gross motor skills. (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334])

**Immediate context**
Motor impairments are prevalent in children with ASD and may impact functional independence. While exercise shows promise for motor skills, no direct evidence from the retrieved data addresses social communication or emotion regulation. Ongoing trials explore occupational therapy and structured walking interventions, but results are not yet available.

## Deep research

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A systematic review (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]) identified **39 studies** (2002–2026) showing behavioral approaches effectively address sleep problems in autistic children. **Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Extended reality (XR)**: A review (Hao et al., *International Journal of Medical Informatics*, 2026 [PMID 42784985]) highlighted **virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR)** as promising tools for ASD support, though no efficacy data were extracted. **Experimental or preliminary**
- **Deep brain stimulation (DBS)**: A 12-month case study (Shi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42759099]) reported **sustained reductions in core ASD symptoms** (e.g., 81.3% improvement in ATEC scores) with bilateral DBS targeting specific brain regions. **Experimental or preliminary**
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A randomized controlled trial (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]) showed **greater short-term reductions in ABC and CARS scores** (behavioral/autism severity) when combined with rehabilitation vs. rehabilitation alone. **Evidence-supported but limited**

### Current evidence
**Motor skills & physical activity**
- **Exercise**: supported by limited evidence dose-response benefits for motor skills (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]). **No direct evidence** for cognitive or social outcomes.
- **Physical activity interventions**: Indirect evidence (Tao et al., 2025 [PMID 39806448]) suggests **mind-body exercise and exergaming** may improve attention/executive function in neurodevelopmental disorders, but **not ASD-specific**. **Indirect evidence**

**Behavioral interventions**
- **Sleep**: **Evidence-supported but limited** for behavioral sleep interventions (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]). **No direct efficacy data** on long-term outcomes.
- **Naturalistic Developmental Behavioral Interventions (NDBIs)**: A pilot RCT (Seng et al., 2026 [PMID 42494078]) found **caregiver-assisted group-based NDBIs** improved social and developmental outcomes in preschoolers, though **one-on-one interventions were more effective for nonverbal skills**. **Emerging**

**Dietary & medical interventions**
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: **Evidence-supported but limited** for short-term behavioral improvements (Liu et al., 2026 [PMID 42763398]). **No long-term or head-to-head data**.

**Technology & environmental**
- **Extended reality (XR)**: **Experimental or preliminary** for ASD support (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]). **No efficacy data retrieved**; focus on modality trends.
- **Nature-based activities**: Qualitative study (Galbraith et al., 2026 [PMID 42603141]) suggested **walking in nature** may enhance communication/calm, but **no quantitative outcomes**. **Indirect evidence**

### What remains under investigation
- **Efficacy**: Long-term outcomes for **ketogenic diet**, **DBS**, and **XR interventions** (no efficacy data retrieved).
- **Effectiveness**: Real-world implementation of **NDBIs** and **sleep interventions** (pilot/feasibility studies only).
- **Feasibility**: **Structured walking interventions** (NCT06659328, completed; results pending) and **oral hygiene training** (NCT07859696, not yet recruiting).
- **Association**: **Sleep spindle characteristics** (Erçelebi et al., 2026 [PMID 42753649]) linked to ASD but **no causal or treatment implications**.
- **Gaps**:
  - **No direct evidence** for social communication or emotion regulation interventions (Chen et al., 2026 [PMID 42486527]: protocol only).
  - **No head-to-head trials** comparing exercise, diet, or behavioral approaches.
  - **Short follow-up** in most studies (e.g., 2 months for ketogenic diet).
