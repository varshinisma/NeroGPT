**Evidence searched on 2026-10-07** (PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA drug labels, web)

**Bottom line**
Exercise interventions show evidence of improving motor skills in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), with dose-response effects reported for overall and gross motor skills (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Most important recent finding**
Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response relationships identified for overall and gross motor skills (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Immediate context**
While motor skill interventions demonstrate efficacy, other domains like social communication and emotion regulation lack direct evidence from completed studies. Protocols for music therapy and occupational therapy-based interventions are under investigation but have not yet established efficacy.

## Deep research

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Exercise**: A dose-response meta-analysis (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]) found significant improvements in **overall, gross, and fine motor skills** in children with ASD, with dose-response effects for overall and gross motor skills. *(Established)*
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A randomized controlled trial (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]) reported **greater short-term reductions in ABC and CARS scores** (measures of behavioral and core ASD symptoms) compared to rehabilitation alone, with acceptable feasibility. *(Evidence-supported but limited)*
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A systematic review (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]) identified **39 studies** (2002–2026) supporting behavioral approaches for sleep problems in autistic children. *(Emerging)*
- **Extended reality (XR)**: A review (Hao et al., *International Journal of Medical Informatics*, 2026 [PMID 42784985]) highlighted **61 VR and 36 AR studies** for ASD, with hybrid systems most common. *(Experimental or preliminary)*
- **Deep brain stimulation (DBS)**: A 12-month case study (Shi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42759099]) showed **sustained reductions in core ASD symptoms** (ATEC, RBS-R, AuBC, CARS-2 scores) with no adverse events. *(Experimental or preliminary)*

### Current evidence
**Established**
- **No established treatments** were retrieved. No guideline-endorsed or consistently high-quality evidence (e.g., systematic reviews of randomized trials) was found for core ASD symptoms (social communication, restricted interests, or repetitive behaviors).

**Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Exercise interventions**: dose-response (exploratory) improvements in **motor skills** (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: Short-term reductions in **behavioral and core ASD symptoms** (Liu et al., 2026 [PMID 42763398]).
- **Caregiver-assisted NDBI**: Feasibility and **preliminary efficacy** in preschoolers (Seng et al., 2026 [PMID 42494078]).

**Emerging**
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: Consistent evidence from 39 studies (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]).
- **XR (VR/AR)**: Growing body of research, but no direct efficacy data (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]).
- **Naturalistic environments**: Parent-reported benefits of **walking in nature** for communication/calm (Galbraith et al., 2026 [PMID 42603141]).

**Experimental or preliminary**
- **Sleep spindles**: Altered spindle characteristics as a **potential neurophysiological marker** (Erçelebi et al., 2026 [PMID 42753649]).
- **Oral hygiene**: Protocols under investigation (e.g., NCT07859696), but no efficacy data.

**Indirect evidence**
- **Feeding difficulties**: FEEDSETUP algorithm for pediatric feeding (Fisberg et al., 2026 [PMID 42716484]) *(different population: not ASD-specific)*.
- **Psychotic features in narcolepsy**: Case-control study noting ASD overlap (Baldini et al., 2026 [PMID 42685448]) *(indirect relevance)*.

**Protocol – no results**
- **Dance program** (Chadha et al., 2026 [PMID 42711081]), **music therapy** (Chen et al., 2026 [PMID 42486527]), **occupational therapy in water** (Kemp et al., 2026 [PMID 42552003]), **oral hygiene techniques** (NCT07859696) – under investigation; no efficacy established.

**Insufficient evidence**
- **Probiotics (Lactobacillus PS128)**: Terminated trial (NCT05307744) due to COVID-19 operational challenges; results posted but **no significant effect reported**.

### Key studies

1. **Wang et al. (2026)**, meta-analysis: “Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD.” (PMID 42758334)
2. **Hao et al. (2026)**, systematic review: “There was a significant increase in XR autism research after 2020, with virtual reality (VR) as the largest modality (n = 61, 53.0%), followed by augmented reality (AR) (n = 36, 31.3%).” (PMID 42784985)
3. **Mazurek et al. (2026)**, systematic review: “Most studies (95%) found that behavioral interventions were effective for at least some sleep outcomes.” (PMID 42685447)
4. **Floríndez et al. (2025)**, systematic review: “Only one study purposefully recruited autistic adults, and no studies included intervention elements tailored to race/ethnicity, culture, and/or socioeconomic status.” (PMID 39056277)
5. **Restoy et al. (2024)**, meta-analysis: “Studies of non-pharmacological interventions showed significant improvement in both ER and ED.” (PMID 38401510)

### What remains under investigation
- **Efficacy**: Head-to-head trials comparing exercise, diet, or XR modalities for **core ASD symptoms** (social communication/restricted interests).
- **Effectiveness**: Real-world implementation of behavioral sleep interventions or NDBI in diverse settings.
- **Feasibility**: Scalability of DBS or high-dose ketogenic diets in routine care.
- **Association**: Long-term outcomes of altered sleep spindles or brain connectivity as biomarkers.
- **Missing**: Trials on **social communication** or **restricted/repetitive behaviors** (no direct evidence retrieved).

---
**Key gaps**: No established treatments for core ASD symptoms; most evidence focuses on **motor skills, sleep, or feeding** (indirect). Ongoing trials (e.g., NCT06659328) await results.
