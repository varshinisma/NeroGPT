## Assistant

**Evidence searched on 2026-10-07** (PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA drug labels, web)

**Bottom line**
Exercise interventions (e.g., dance, occupational therapy) show evidence improving motor skills in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), with dose-response effects reported for overall and gross motor function (Wang et al., *European journal of pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]). Structured programs like music therapy and water competency interventions are under investigation for broader developmental outcomes, though no direct efficacy data are yet available.

**Most important recent finding**
Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response relationships identified for overall and gross motor skills (Wang et al., *European journal of pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Immediate context**
While motor skill interventions demonstrate efficacy, protocols for social communication (e.g., music therapy) and adaptive behaviors (e.g., occupational therapy in water safety) remain exploratory or under investigation. No direct evidence supports their efficacy for core ASD symptoms.

## Deep research

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Exercise**: A **multilevel meta-analysis** (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]) found supported by limited evidence dose-response improvements in **motor skills** (overall, gross, and fine) in children with ASD, with higher doses linked to greater gains.
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A **randomized controlled trial** (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]) reported **short-term reductions** in ABC and CARS scores (behavioral and symptom severity) when combined with rehabilitation, but **no long-term efficacy data** was provided.
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A **systematic review** (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]) reported **efficacy** of behavioral strategies for sleep problems in autistic children, though no single protocol was highlighted.
- **Extended reality (XR)**: A **systematic review** (Hao et al., *International Journal of Medical Informatics*, 2026 [PMID 42784985]) identified **promising but inconsistent** findings for VR/AR in ASD, with hybrid systems most studied but no direct efficacy evidence.
- **Deep brain stimulation (DBS)**: A **12-month case study** (Shi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42759099]) showed **sustained core symptom reduction** in a single child with severe ASD, but **no generalizable evidence** due to case-specific design.

### Current evidence
**Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Motor skills**: Exercise interventions are **consistently shown** to improve motor function in ASD (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: **Systematic review** supports behavioral strategies for sleep problems (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]).

- **Naturalistic Developmental Behavioral Interventions (NDBIs)**: A **pilot RCT** (Seng et al., *Autism*, 2026 [PMID 42494078]) demonstrated **feasibility and preliminary efficacy** in preschoolers, though group-based caregiver-assisted models showed **mixed results** for nonverbal development.
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: **Short-term behavioral improvements** observed (Liu et al., 2026 [PMID 42763398]), but **no long-term or mechanistic data** provided.

**Emerging**
- **Extended reality (XR)**: **Growing research interest** (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]), but **no preliminary efficacy** supported by limited evidence for ASD outcomes.

**Experimental or preliminary**
- **Sleep spindle characteristics**: Observational study (Erçelebi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42753649]) linked **reduced spindle activity** to ASD, suggesting a **potential neurophysiological marker** but no therapeutic implication.
- **Social communication via music therapy**: **Protocol-only** (Chen et al., 2026 [PMID 42486527]); **no results** published.

**Indirect evidence**
- **Feeding difficulties (FEEDSETUP)**: A **review** (Fisberg et al., *Jornal de Pediatria*, 2026 [PMID 42716484]) proposed a structured approach for **pediatric feeding difficulties**, but **not specific to ASD**.

**Protocol – no results**
- **Dance program (AllPlay Dance)**: RCT protocol (Chadha et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42711081]) for motor skills via dance; **results pending**.
- **Occupational therapy in water (AquOTic)**: Hybrid effectiveness-implementation trial (Kemp et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42552003]) for water competency; **no efficacy data**.

**Insufficient evidence**
- **Prenatal antiseizure medication (ASM)**: Observational study (Hernández-Díaz et al., *NEJM*, 2024 [PMID 38507750]) linked **valproate/topiramate exposure** to higher ASD risk, but **no treatment implications** for existing ASD.

### Key studies

1. **Wang et al. (2026)**, meta-analysis: “Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD.” (PMID 42758334)
2. **Hao et al. (2026)**, systematic review: “There was a significant increase in XR autism research after 2020, with virtual reality (VR) as the largest modality (n = 61, 53.0%), followed by augmented reality (AR) (n = 36, 31.3%).” (PMID 42784985)
3. **Mazurek et al. (2026)**, systematic review: “Most studies (95%) found that behavioral interventions were effective for at least some sleep outcomes.” (PMID 42685447)
4. **Restoy et al. (2024)**, meta-analysis: “Studies of non-pharmacological interventions showed significant improvement in both ER and ED.” (PMID 38401510)
5. **Sandbank et al. (2023)**, meta-analysis: “When effects were then restricted to exclude those at high risk of detection bias, only one significant summary effect was estimated-naturalistic developmental behavioral interventions on measures of diagnostic characteristics of autism (0.30, 0.03 to 0.57; P=0.03).” (PMID 37963634)

### What remains under investigation
- **Efficacy**: Head-to-head comparisons of **exercise vs. behavioral interventions**, **long-term ketogenic diet effects**, and **XR vs. traditional therapy** for ASD outcomes.
- **Effectiveness**: Real-world implementation of **NDBIs** and **sleep interventions** in diverse settings.
- **Feasibility**: Scalability of **DBS** and **water-based occupational therapy** (AquOTic).
- **Association**: Role of **sleep spindles** (Erçelebi et al., 2026 [PMID 42753649]) as biomarkers or therapeutic targets.
- **Missing**: **Longitudinal data** on behavioral interventions, **mechanistic studies** for XR, and **cost-effectiveness analyses** for emerging treatments.
