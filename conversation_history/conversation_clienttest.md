**Evidence searched on 2026-10-06** (PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA drug labels, web)

**Bottom line**
Exercise interventions show a dose-response benefit for motor skills in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), with significant improvements in overall, gross, and fine motor skills (Wang et al., *European journal of pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]). Behavioral and occupational therapy protocols (e.g., dance, water competency) are under investigation for broader developmental outcomes, though no direct efficacy results are yet available.

**Most important recent finding**
Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with identified dose-response relationships for overall and gross motor skills (Wang et al., *European journal of pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Immediate context**
No direct evidence yet supports these interventions for core ASD symptoms.

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Exercise**: A dose-response meta-analysis showed significant improvements in **overall, gross, and fine motor skills** in children with ASD, with stronger effects at higher exercise doses (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A randomized controlled trial reported **greater short-term reductions in ABC and CARS scores** (measures of behavior and autism severity) compared to rehabilitation alone, with acceptable feasibility (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]).
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A systematic review identified **39 studies (2002–2026)** supporting behavioral approaches for sleep problems in autistic children (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]).

### Current evidence
**Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Behavioral interventions for sleep**: Systematic review reports behavioral strategies improve sleep outcomes in autistic children (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]).

- **Exercise**: Meta-analysis demonstrates **dose-response (exploratory) improvements in motor skills** (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Naturalistic Developmental Behavioral Interventions (NDBIs)**: Pilot RCT shows **feasibility and preliminary efficacy** for caregiver-assisted group-based NDBI, though one-on-one interventions yielded stronger nonverbal gains (Seng et al., *Autism: International Journal of Research and Practice*, 2026 [PMID 42494078]).

**Emerging**
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: RCT suggests **short-term reductions in autism severity scores** when combined with rehabilitation, but long-term data are lacking (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]).
- **Emotion regulation**: Meta-analysis highlights **non-pharmacological interventions** (e.g., therapy) improve emotion regulation/dysregulation in ASD (Restoy et al., *Clinical Psychology Review*, 2024 [PMID 38401510]).

**Experimental or preliminary**
- **Music therapy**: Protocol for a meta-analysis exploring **dosage effects on social communication/emotion regulation** (Chen et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42486527]).
- **Occupational therapy (water competency)**: Hybrid effectiveness-implementation trial protocol for drowning prevention in ASD (Kemp et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42552003]).

**Indirect evidence**
- **Feeding difficulties (FEEDSETUP)**: Algorithm for **integrated, individualized care** in pediatric feeding disorders (Fisberg et al., *Jornal de Pediatria*, 2026 [PMID 42716484]) *(population: broader feeding difficulties, not ASD-specific)*.
- **Genetic/environmental overlap**: Twin study shows **shared genetic/environmental factors** between ASD, anxiety, and depression (Han et al., *Journal of Affective Disorders*, 2026 [PMID 42508582]) *(population: broader neurodevelopmental traits)*. *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*

**Protocol – no results**
- **Dance program (AllPlay Dance)**: RCT protocol for community-based dance intervention in ASD (Chadha et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42711081]).

**Insufficient evidence**
- **Omega-3/inositol vs. NAC**: RCT in emotionally dysregulated youth (with/without ASD) showed **trends toward benefit** for depression/mania, but ASD-specific data were limited (Wozniak et al., *Journal of Affective Disorders*, 2026 [PMID 42462928]) *(population: broader emotional dysregulation)*. *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*

### Key studies

1. **Wang et al. (2026)**, *Meta-Analysis*: Exercise significantly improved **overall, gross, and fine motor skills** in ASD, with dose-response effects (PMID 42758334).
2. **Liu et al. (2026)**, *Randomized Controlled Trial*: Modified ketogenic diet + rehabilitation reduced **ABC and CARS scores** more than rehabilitation alone (PMID 42763398).
3. **Seng et al. (2026)**, *Randomized Controlled Trial*: Caregiver-assisted group-based NDBI showed **feasibility and preliminary social/nonverbal gains** (PMID 42494078).
4. **Mazurek et al. (2026)**, *Systematic Review*: **39 studies (2002–2026)** reported behavioral sleep interventions’ efficacy in ASD (PMID 42685447).
5. **Restoy et al. (2024)**, *Meta-Analysis*: Non-pharmacological interventions improved **emotion regulation/dysregulation** in ASD (PMID 38401510).

### What remains under investigation
- **Efficacy**: Long-term outcomes of modified ketogenic diet (Liu et al., 2026 [PMID 42763398]); head-to-head comparisons of NDBI formats (Seng et al., 2026 [PMID 42494078]).
- **Effectiveness**: Real-world implementation of dance (Chadha et al., 2026 [PMID 42711081]) or water competency (Kemp et al., 2026 [PMID 42552003]) programs.
- **Feasibility**: Scalability of caregiver-assisted interventions (Seng et al., 2026 [PMID 42494078]).
- **Association**: Genetic/environmental mechanisms linking ASD to anxiety/depression (Han et al., 2026 [PMID 42508582]) *(indirect population)*.
- **Surrogate endpoints**: Motor skills (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]) and emotion regulation (Restoy et al., 2024 [PMID 38401510]) as proxies for broader ASD outcomes.

---
**Gaps**:
- No **direct evidence** for music therapy, occupational therapy (water), or omega-3/inositol in ASD-specific populations.
- **Short-term focus**: Most studies report ≤2 months (e.g., Liu et al., 2026 [PMID 42763398]; Seng et al., 2026 [PMID 42494078]).
- **Subgroup analyses**: Limited data on severity, age, or comorbidity stratification.
- **Pipeline failures**: Terminated trial (NCT05307744) due to operational challenges.
