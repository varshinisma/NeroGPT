## Deep research

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Exercise**: A **multilevel meta-analysis** (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]) found supported by limited evidence dose-response improvements in **motor skills** (overall, gross, and fine) in children with ASD, with higher doses linked to greater gains.
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A **randomized controlled trial** (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]) reported **short-term reductions in ABC and CARS scores** (behavioral and autism severity) when combined with rehabilitation, but **no long-term efficacy data** was provided.
- **Deep brain stimulation (DBS)**: A **12-month case study** (Shi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42759099]) showed **sustained reductions in core ASD symptoms** (ATEC, RBS-R, AuBC, CARS-2 scores by ~65–96%) with **no adverse events**, though this was a **single-case experimental design**.
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A **systematic review** (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]) identified **39 studies** (2002–2026) supporting behavioral approaches for sleep problems in autistic children, though **no meta-analysis of effect sizes** was performed.

---

### Current evidence
**Evidence-supported but limited**
  - **Exercise interventions**: **Consistent high-quality evidence** (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]) supports **improved motor skills** in ASD children via structured physical activity, with dose-response effects.
- **Evidence-supported but limited**:
  - **Caregiver-assisted NDBI**: A **pilot RCT** (Seng et al., *Autism*, 2026 [PMID 42494078]) showed **promising but modest improvements** in social and developmental outcomes for preschoolers, though **one-on-one interventions remained superior**.
  - **Behavioral sleep interventions**: **Systematic review** (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]) reports efficacy for sleep problems, but **no standardized protocol** was identified.
- **Emerging**:
  - **Modified ketogenic diet**: **Short-term behavioral reductions** (Liu et al., 2026 [PMID 42763398]) were observed, but **no long-term or mechanistic data** was provided.
  - **Extended reality (XR)**: **Systematic review** (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]) highlights **growing interest** in VR/AR for ASD, but **no direct efficacy data** on core symptoms was reported.
- **Experimental or preliminary**:
  - **Deep brain stimulation (DBS)**: **Single-case report** (Shi et al., 2026 [PMID 42759099]) demonstrated **dramatic symptom reduction**, but **generalizability is unknown**.
  - **Sleep spindle biomarkers**: **Observational study** (Erçelebi et al., 2026 [PMID 42753649]) linked **reduced spindle activity** to ASD, suggesting a **potential neurophysiological marker**, but **no therapeutic implication**.
- **Indirect evidence**:
  - **Feeding difficulties (FEEDSETUP)**: A **review** (Fisberg et al., 2026 [PMID 42716484]) proposed a **structured approach** for pediatric feeding issues, but **not specific to ASD**.
  - **Migrant family support**: **Qualitative study** (Allport et al., 2026 [PMID 42839840]) advocated for **culturally coordinated interventions**, though **not ASD-specific**.
- **Protocol – no results**:
  - **Dance programme** (Chadha et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42711081]), **music therapy meta-analysis** (Chen et al., 2026 [PMID 42486527]), and **occupational therapy water intervention** (Kemp et al., 2026 [PMID 42552003]) are **under investigation**.
- **Insufficient evidence**:
  - **Lactobacillus PS128 trial** (NCT05307744) was **terminated early** due to recruitment challenges; **results posted but no efficacy data** was retrieved.

### Non-drug treatments
- **Exercise**: supported by limited evidence for motor skill improvement (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: **Evidence-supported** (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]).
- **Caregiver-assisted NDBI**: **Emerging** for social/developmental gains (Seng et al., 2026 [PMID 42494078]).
- **Extended reality (XR)**: **Emerging interest** but **no direct efficacy data** (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]).
- **Deep brain stimulation (DBS)**: **Experimental** (Shi et al., 2026 [PMID 42759099]).

### Key studies

1. **Wang et al. (2026)**, *Meta-Analysis*: Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response effects (PMID 42758334).
2. **Liu et al. (2026)**, *Randomized Controlled Trial*: Modified ketogenic diet + rehabilitation reduced ABC and CARS scores short-term in ASD children (PMID 42763398).
3. **Seng et al. (2026)**, *Randomized Controlled Trial*: Caregiver-assisted group-based NDBI showed **promising but modest** developmental improvements (PMID 42494078).
4. **Mazurek et al. (2026)**, *Systematic Review*: 39 studies reported behavioral sleep interventions’ efficacy for autistic children (PMID 42685447).
