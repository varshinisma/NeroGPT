## Clinical summary

**Bottom line**
The retrieved evidence supports **exercise interventions** (improving motor skills) and **behavioral therapies** (e.g., naturalistic developmental interventions) as effective approaches for children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD). A **modified ketogenic diet combined with rehabilitation** showed short-term reductions in behavioral and symptom severity, but this remains a targeted intervention. **No evidence was found for pharmacological treatments or broad social/communication outcomes** in the retrieved data.

**Most important recent finding**
Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response relationships identified for overall and gross motor skills [1].

**Immediate context**
Behavioral interventions (e.g., caregiver-assisted naturalistic developmental behavioral interventions) show promise for early developmental gains, while dietary approaches (e.g., modified ketogenic diet) may reduce specific symptoms but lack long-term efficacy data. **No evidence supports broad cognitive or social communication improvements** in the retrieved studies.

## Latest findings
- **2026 Dec**: Observational study [2] – Altered sleep spindle characteristics were significantly associated with autism spectrum disorder (ASD) in children, with reduced amplitude, number, and density but longer duration.
- **2026 Sep 18**: Meta-analysis [1] – Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response effects for overall and gross motor skills.
- **2026 Sep 11**: Randomized controlled trial [3] – A modified ketogenic diet combined with rehabilitation training showed greater short-term reductions in behavioral and symptom severity scores (ABC, CARS) than rehabilitation alone.

## What remains under investigation
- **Efficacy**: Head-to-head comparisons of behavioral interventions (e.g., NDBIs vs. traditional therapy) for long-term outcomes in ASD.
- **Effectiveness**: Real-world effectiveness of extended reality (XR) interventions in diverse clinical settings.
- **Feasibility**: Long-term adherence and scalability of modified ketogenic diets in ASD populations.
- **Association**: Neurophysiological markers (e.g., sleep spindle characteristics) as predictors of treatment response in ASD.
- **Exploratory**: Dose-response relationships for pharmacological agents (e.g., cannabinoids) in core symptom domains.
