## Deep research

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Exercise interventions**: A dose-response meta-analysis (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]) showed significant improvements in **overall, gross, and fine motor skills** in children with ASD, with an exploratory dose-response relationship reported. *(Established)*
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A systematic review (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]) identified **39 studies** (2002–2023) demonstrating efficacy of behavioral sleep interventions in autistic children under 18. *(Evidence-supported but limited)*
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A randomized controlled trial (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]) reported **greater short-term reductions in ABC and CARS scores** (measures of behavior and autism severity) with a modified ketogenic diet + rehabilitation vs. rehabilitation alone. *(Emerging)*
- **Caregiver-assisted NDBI**: A pilot RCT (Seng et al., *Autism: International Journal of Research and Practice*, 2026 [PMID 42494078]) found **promising feasibility and preliminary efficacy** for group-based naturalistic developmental behavioral interventions, though one-on-one care showed stronger nonverbal gains. *(Emerging)*
- **Omega-3 + inositol vs. NAC**: A RCT (Wozniak et al., *Journal of Affective Disorders*, 2026 [PMID 42462928]) showed **significant improvements in SRS and BRIEF scores** (social and behavioral regulation) for emotionally dysregulated youth with/without autism traits, with trends favoring omega-3 for depression. *(Indirect evidence; population: emotionally dysregulated youth)*

### Current evidence
**Established**
- **No evidence retrieved** for guideline-endorsed or high-quality systematic reviews directly addressing autism treatment in children.

**Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: Systematic review (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]) reports efficacy across 39 studies for autistic children under 18. *(Direct; population: autistic children <18)*
- **Emotion regulation interventions**: Meta-analysis (Restoy et al., *Clinical Psychology Review*, 2024 [PMID 38401510]) showed **significant improvements in emotion regulation/dysregulation** via non-pharmacological methods. *(Direct; population: children/adolescents with ASD)*

**Emerging**
- **Exercise**: Dose-response meta-analysis (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]) establishes **motor skill improvements** with structured physical activity. *(Direct; population: children with ASD)*
- **Dietary interventions**: RCT (Liu et al., 2026 [PMID 42763398]) suggests **short-term behavioral benefits** from a modified ketogenic diet + rehabilitation. *(Direct; population: young children with ASD)*
- **NDBI adaptations**: Pilot RCT (Seng et al., 2026 [PMID 42494078]) supports **feasibility of group-based caregiver-assisted NDBI**, though individual gains were larger. *(Direct; population: autistic preschoolers)*

**Experimental or preliminary**
- **Music therapy protocol**: Systematic review/meta-analysis protocol (Chen et al., 2026 [PMID 42486527]) aims to assess **dose-response effects on social communication/emotion regulation** (results pending). *(Direct; protocol only)*
- **Occupational therapy (water competency)**: Hybrid effectiveness-implementation trial protocol (Kemp et al., 2026 [PMID 42552003]) tests drowning-prevention interventions (results pending). *(Direct; protocol only)*
- **Case study**: Single case report (Hitomi et al., *Case Reports in Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42781187]) highlights **diagnostic delays in ASD-linked Crohn’s disease** (not a treatment). *(Direct; population: adolescent with ASD)*

**Indirect evidence**
- **Genetic/environmental overlap**: Twin meta-analysis (Han et al., 2026 [PMID 42508582]) links autistic traits to **anxiety/depression** (not ASD treatment). *(Population: general ASD traits)* *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*
- **Nutraceuticals for mood**: RCT (Wozniak et al., 2026 [PMID 42462928]) tested omega-3/inositol vs. NAC in **emotionally dysregulated youth** (some with ASD traits), showing mixed efficacy. *(Population: youth with emotional dysregulation)* *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*

### Key studies

1. **Wang et al. (2026)**, meta-analysis: “Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD.” (PMID 42758334)
2. **Mazurek et al. (2026)**, systematic review: “Most studies (95%) found that behavioral interventions were effective for at least some sleep outcomes.” (PMID 42685447)
3. **Restoy et al. (2024)**, meta-analysis: “Studies of non-pharmacological interventions showed significant improvement in both ER and ED.” (PMID 38401510)
4. **Sandbank et al. 5. **Zeidan et al. (2022)**, systematic review: “Prevalence estimates increased over time and varied greatly within and across sociodemographic groups.” (PMID 35238171)
