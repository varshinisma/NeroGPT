**Bottom line**
Exercise interventions (e.g., dance or structured walking) show evidence improving motor skills in children with autism, with dose-response effects reported for overall and gross motor skills (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]). Behavioral and occupational therapy-based programs (e.g., water competency training) are also under investigation for functional outcomes, though no direct efficacy data are yet available.

**Most important recent finding**
Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response relationships identified for overall and gross motor skills. (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334])

**Immediate context**
Motor impairments are prevalent in children with ASD and may impact functional independence. While behavioral and environmental interventions (e.g., dance, occupational therapy) are promising, no direct evidence yet supports their efficacy for core ASD symptoms like social communication or repetitive behaviors.

## Deep research

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Exercise interventions**: A dose-response meta-analysis (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]) showed significant improvements in **overall, gross, and fine motor skills** in children with ASD, with dose-response effects for overall and gross motor skills. supported by limited evidence.
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A randomized controlled trial (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]) found **greater short-term reductions in ABC and CARS scores** (measures of behavior and autism severity) compared to rehabilitation alone, with acceptable feasibility. **Evidence-supported but limited** (short-term follow-up).
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A systematic review (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]) identified **39 studies** supporting behavioral approaches for sleep problems in autistic children, though no single intervention was universally effective. **Emerging** (heterogeneous evidence).
- **Extended reality (XR)**: A review (Hao et al., *International Journal of Medical Informatics*, 2026 [PMID 42784985]) highlighted **61 VR and 36 AR studies**, predominantly focusing on training/education, but no direct efficacy data were synthesized. **Experimental or preliminary**.
- **Deep brain stimulation (DBS)**: A 12-month case study (Shi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42759099]) reported **sustained reductions in core ASD symptoms** (ATEC, RBS-R, AuBC, CARS-2 scores) with no adverse events. **Experimental or preliminary** (single-case design).

### Current evidence
**Evidence-supported but limited**

- **Modified ketogenic diet** (Liu et al., 2026 [PMID 42763398]): Short-term reductions in behavior and autism severity scores, but **no long-term data** or comparison to standard therapies.
- **Caregiver-assisted group-based NDBI** (Seng et al., *Autism*, 2026 [PMID 42494078]): Promising for **social and developmental gains** in preschoolers, though one-on-one interventions remained superior for nonverbal outcomes. **Feasibility established**; efficacy requires larger trials.

**Emerging**
- **Behavioral sleep interventions** (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]): Broad support for behavioral approaches, but **no head-to-head comparisons** or standardized protocols.
- **Extended reality (XR)** (Hao et al., 2026 [PMID 42784985]): Growing interest in VR/AR for training/education, but **no direct evidence of clinical benefit** for ASD core symptoms.
- **Naturalistic play interventions** (e.g., dance programs like AllPlay Dance; Chadha et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42711081] protocol): **Under investigation**; no results yet.

**Experimental or preliminary**
- **Sleep spindle biomarkers** (Erçelebi et al., *Clinical Neurophysiology*, 2026 [PMID 42753649]): Altered spindle characteristics may **predict ASD**, but no treatment implications yet.
- **Occupational therapy in water** (Kemp et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42552003] protocol): **Under investigation**; no efficacy data.

**Indirect evidence**
- **Feeding difficulties (FEEDSETUP)** (Fisberg et al., *Jornal de Pediatria*, 2026 [PMID 42716484]): An **integrated approach** for pediatric feeding issues, but **not specific to ASD core symptoms**.
- **Prenatal antiseizure medication risk** (Hernández-Díaz et al., *NEJM*, 2024 [PMID 38507750]): **Association between valproate/topiramate exposure and ASD risk**, but **not a treatment strategy**.

**Protocol – no results**
- **Music therapy dosage** (Chen et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42486527]): Systematic review protocol for **social communication/emotion regulation**; results pending.
- **Dance programs** (Chadha et al., 2026 [PMID 42711081]), **water competency** (Kemp et al., 2026 [PMID 42552003]), **oral hygiene** (NCT07859696): **Under investigation**; no efficacy established.

**Insufficient evidence**
- **No retrieved data** on:
  - Pharmacological treatments (e.g., oxytocin, risperidone, SSRIs) for core ASD symptoms.
  - Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) or speech therapy efficacy.
  - Long-term outcomes of behavioral interventions.
