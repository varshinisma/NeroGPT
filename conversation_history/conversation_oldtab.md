**Evidence searched on 2026-10-06** (PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA drug labels, web)

**Bottom line**
Exercise interventions show a dose-response effect improving motor skills in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), with significant gains in overall, gross, and fine motor function (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Most important recent finding**
Exercise significantly improved overall, gross, and fine motor skills in children with ASD, with dose-response relationships identified for overall and gross motor skills (Wang et al., *European Journal of Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42758334]).

**Immediate context**
While motor skill interventions (e.g., dance, occupational therapy) are under investigation, **no direct evidence** from retrieved sources addresses core ASD symptoms like social communication or repetitive behaviors. Protocols for music therapy and water competency interventions exist but have not yet reported efficacy.

### Latest findings
**2026**
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: A randomized controlled trial (RCT) in 2026 found that a modified ketogenic diet combined with rehabilitation training reduced ABC and CARS scores more than rehabilitation alone in young children with ASD, with short-term feasibility (Liu et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026 [PMID 42763398]). **Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: A 2026 systematic review identified 39 studies (2002–2023) supporting behavioral sleep interventions for autistic children under 18, though no direct efficacy comparisons were reported (Mazurek et al., *Sleep Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42685447]). **Emerging**

### Current evidence
#### **Established**
- **No established treatments**: No guideline-endorsed or consistently high-quality evidence was retrieved for core ASD symptoms (e.g., social communication, restricted/repetitive behaviors).

#### **Evidence-supported but limited**
- **Naturalistic Developmental Behavioral Interventions (NDBIs)**: A 2026 pilot RCT found caregiver-assisted group-based NDBI improved developmental and social outcomes in preschoolers, though one-on-one interventions showed stronger gains (Seng et al., *Autism: International Journal of Research and Practice*, 2026 [PMID 42494078]).

#### **Emerging**
- **Exercise**: Dose-response effects on motor skills (Wang et al., 2026 [PMID 42758334]).
- **Modified ketogenic diet**: Short-term reductions in ASD symptom severity (Liu et al., 2026 [PMID 42763398]).
- **Behavioral sleep interventions**: Consistent but heterogeneous support (Mazurek et al., 2026 [PMID 42685447]).
- **Omega-3 + inositol/NAC**: Indirect evidence (youth with emotional dysregulation) suggested potential for depression/mania (Wozniak et al., *Journal of Affective Disorders*, 2026 [PMID 42462928]).

#### **Experimental or preliminary**
- **Music therapy**: Protocol for a 2026 meta-analysis on social communication/emotion regulation (Chen et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42486527]).
- **Occupational therapy (water competency)**: Hybrid effectiveness-implementation trial protocol (Kemp et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42552003]).
- **Feeding difficulties**: Indirect evidence for a structured evaluation/treatment approach (Fisberg et al., *Jornal de Pediatria*, 2026 [PMID 42716484]).

#### **Indirect evidence**
- **Genetic/environmental overlap**: Twin studies linked autistic traits to anxiety/depression (Han et al., *Journal of Affective Disorders*, 2026 [PMID 42508582]). *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*
- **Prenatal antiseizure meds**: Valproate/topiramate exposure increased ASD risk (Hernández-Díaz et al., *NEJM*, 2024 [PMID 38507750]).

#### **Protocol – no results**
- Dance program (Chadha et al., *BMJ Open*, 2026 [PMID 42711081]).
- Lactobacillus plantarum PS128 (NCT05307744, terminated).

#### **Insufficient evidence**
- **Nature walks**: Qualitative parent experiences suggested developmental benefits but no quantitative efficacy (Galbraith et al., *International Journal of Qualitative Studies on Health and Well-being*, 2026 [PMID 42603141]).
- **Emotional dysregulation prediction**: Dataset for machine learning (Turabee et al., *MethodsX*, 2026 [PMID 42569523]).

- **Modified ketogenic diet**: Short-term symptom reduction (Liu et al., 2026 [PMID 42763398]).

### Key studies

1. **Wang et al. (2026)**, Meta-analysis: Exercise significantly improved motor skills in ASD, with dose-response effects (*European Journal of Pediatrics*, PMID 42758334).
2. **Liu et al. (2026)**, Randomized controlled trial: Modified ketogenic diet + rehabilitation reduced ASD symptom severity (*BMC Pediatrics*, PMID 42763398).
3. **Seng et al. (2026)**, Randomized controlled trial: Caregiver-assisted group NDBI improved developmental/social outcomes (*Autism: International Journal of Research and Practice*, PMID 42494078).
4. **Mazurek et al. (2026)**, Systematic review: 39 studies supported behavioral sleep interventions (*Sleep Medicine*, PMID 42685447).
5. **Hernández-Díaz et al. (2024)**, Case-control study: Prenatal valproate/topiramate exposure increased ASD risk (*NEJM*, PMID 38507750).

### What remains under investigation
- **Efficacy**: Head-to-head trials of exercise, diet, or NDBIs vs. standard care.
- **Effectiveness**: Long-term outcomes of behavioral/sleep interventions in routine care.
- **Feasibility**: Scalability of group-based NDBIs or ketogenic diets.
- **Association**: Role of genetic/environmental factors in treatment response (indirect evidence).
- **Ongoing trials**:
  - **NCT06659328** (completed): Structured walking intervention for gait parameters (results pending).
  - **NCT07859696** (not yet recruiting): Oral hygiene skills in autistic children (peer modeling).

**Uncertainty**: No retrieved evidence for core ASD symptoms (e.g., social communication), reliance on short-term outcomes, and indirect/preliminary findings for most interventions.
