# How to treat cancer in adults?

**Evidence searched on 2026-10-09** (PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA drug labels, web)

## Clinical summary

**Bottom line**
The retrieved evidence does not provide direct clinical guidance on treating adult cancer, as no published studies or efficacy results were found. The only data available are **three ongoing or planned trials** (e.g., NCT07578649, a Phase 3 study of PF-08634404 + pembrolizumab + chemotherapy for advanced endometrial cancer, *not yet recruiting*) and **FDA approval announcements** (e.g., zenocutuzumab-zbco for NRG1-fusion NSCLC, *FDA, 2026 URL*), which do not establish broad treatment approaches.

**Most important recent finding**
FDA granted accelerated approval to **zenocutuzumab-zbco (Bizengri®)** for adults with advanced, unresectable, or metastatic NSCLC harboring a *NRG1* gene fusion (FDA, 2026) [https://www.lungcancerresearchfoundation.org/research/why-research/treatment-advances].

**Immediate context**
No direct evidence from published studies or completed trials was retrieved to support general cancer treatment strategies in adults. Approvals (e.g., tarlatamab for small cell lung cancer, *FDA, 2025*) are limited to specific biomarkers or cancer types and do not apply broadly. Ongoing trials explore targeted combinations (e.g., immunotherapy + chemotherapy), but results are pending.

## Latest findings
- **2027 Feb 1**: *Wang et al.* (GC-MS-based exhaled VOC diagnostic model for distinguishing early-stage lung cancer from controls) – A single-center study identified early metabolic alterations in exhaled breath that distinguish early-stage lung cancer from controls, but requires external validation for generalizability [3]. *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*
- **2027 Feb 1**: *Li et al.* (Peptide-functionalized magnetic nanobeads for EpCAM-targeted CTC isolation and immunotherapy efficacy evaluation in liver cancer) – A peptide-nanobead liquid biopsy strategy effectively stratifies liver cancer patients likely to benefit from PD-1 inhibitors, with cTPShigh as an independent predictor of progression-free and overall survival [1]. *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*
- **2027 Jan 15**: *Odrzywolski et al.* (Extracellular vesicle-derived FTIR spectroscopy for colorectal cancer detection) – FTIR spectroscopy of extracellular vesicles (EVs) achieved 95.6–97.8% accuracy in distinguishing colorectal cancer patients from controls, outperforming plasma-based methods [4]. *(indirect evidence: different or related population)*
- **Advance publication (issue dated 2027 Jan)**: *Allen et al.* (Hypofractionated proton radiotherapy for high-risk lung cancer) – Hypofractionated proton radiotherapy demonstrated 1- and 2-year local control rates of 83.1% and 77.4%, respectively, in a high-risk lung cancer cohort [2].

## Key studies

1. **Kalinsky et al. (2025)**, randomized controlled trial: “At the primary analysis (258 events), the hazard ratio (HR) was 0.73 (95% CI, 0.57 to 0.95; nominal P = .017), with median PFS 6.0 (95% CI, 5.6 to 8.6) versus 5.3 (95% CI, 3.7 to 5.6) months and 6-month PFS rates of 50% and 37% in the abemaciclib + fulvestrant and placebo + fulvestrant arms, respectively.” [7]
2. **Janjigian et al. (2025)**, randomized controlled trial: “Two-year event-free survival (Kaplan-Meier estimate) was 67.4% among the participants in the durvalumab group and 58.5% among those in the placebo group (hazard ratio for event or death, 0.71; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.58 to 0.86; P<0.001).” [8]
3. **Pusztai et al. (2024)**, randomized controlled trial: “The corresponding hazard ratios (95% confidence intervals) for EFS were 0.70 (0.38-1.31), 0.92 (0.39-2.20), 0.52 (0.32-0.82), and 1.24 (0.69-2.23).” [9]
4. **Qiu et al. (2024)**, randomized controlled trial: “Of 1657 patients screened between 13 December 2018 and 9 February 2021, 660 were ineligible due to not meeting the eligibility criteria, withdrawal of consent, adverse events, or other reasons.” [10]
5. **Herrera et al. (2024)**, randomized controlled trial: “Of 994 patients who underwent randomization, 970 were included in the intention-to-treat population for efficacy analyses.” [11]

## What remains under investigation
- **Immunotherapy biomarkers**: Liquid biopsy strategies (e.g., peptide-nanobeads) to predict PD-1 inhibitor response in liver cancer are under investigation, but efficacy remains exploratory (2027).
- **Radiotherapy fractionation**: Optimal hypofractionated proton therapy regimens for high-risk lung cancer are being evaluated, with long-term outcomes pending (2027).
- **Exercise in geriatric oncology**: While observational data suggest benefits, randomized trials are needed to confirm efficacy in improving prognosis for older cancer patients (2026).
- **Non-invasive diagnostics**: Breath-based (VOCs) and EV spectroscopy for early cancer detection are promising but require multicenter validation (2027).
- **Complementary therapies**: Music therapy shows symptom management promise, but its role in routine cancer care remains understudied in large-scale trials.
