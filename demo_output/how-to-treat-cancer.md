**Bottom line**
Cancer treatment involves **multidisciplinary approaches**, including **systemic therapies (e.g., targeted drugs, immunotherapies)**, **supportive interventions (e.g., behavioral speech therapy, mHealth symptom management)**, and **perioperative management (e.g., graphene-based materials, lymphedema therapies)**. Evidence supports behavioral speech therapy for postoperative pain and function in lung cancer (Zhang et al., *Supportive Care Cancer*, 2026 [PMID 42611082]) and mHealth apps for symptom management in advanced cancer (Wells-Di et al., *JMIR Cancer*, 2026 [PMID 42748353]).

**Key result:** Sizi Powder external application combined with TDP therapy significantly reduced upper limb lymphedema swelling, alleviated symptom burden, and improved functional outcomes after breast cancer surgery (Zeng et al., *Medicine*, 2026 [PMID 42826302]).

## Deep research

### Latest findings
**2027**
- **Emerging**: Mucosal impedance measurements (MI-H and MI-L) distinguish early gastric cancer stages (pT1a vs. pT1b) and ulceration status (pUL0 vs. pUL1) (Miyashiro et al., DEN open, 2027 [PMID 42781318]).

**2026**
- **Evidence-supported but limited**: Psychosocial interventions improve neurocognitive outcomes in children/adolescents with cancer (SMD: 0.51 [95% CI: 0.32, 0.69]) (Paterson et al., Supportive care in cancer, 2026 [PMID 42766188]).
- **Evidence-supported but limited**: Behavioral speech therapy (BST) reduces postoperative pain and improves physical function in lung cancer patients (Zhang et al., Supportive care in cancer, 2026 [PMID 42611082]).
- **Emerging**: Home-based exercise (exCR) improves cardiorespiratory fitness (CRF) and health-related quality of life (HRQoL) in cancer patients (Winnige et al., Annals of medicine, 2026 [PMID 42811812]).
- **Experimental or preliminary**: Sizi Powder + TDP therapy may reduce swelling and improve function in upper limb lymphedema post-breast cancer surgery (Zeng et al., Medicine, 2026 [PMID 42826302]).

**2026 (Drugs)**
- **Evidence-supported but limited**: Trastuzumab deruxtecan + pertuzumab extends progression-free survival (PFS) in HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer (median PFS: 40.7 vs. 26.9 months) (Tolaney et al., NEJM, 2026 [PMID 41160818]).

### Current evidence
**Non-drug treatments**
- **Evidence-supported but limited**: Psychosocial interventions (e.g., cognitive therapies) improve neurocognitive outcomes in pediatric/adolescent cancer patients (Paterson et al., 2026 [PMID 42766188]).
- **Evidence-supported but limited**:
  - Behavioral speech therapy (BST) reduces postoperative pain and enhances physical function in lung cancer patients (Zhang et al., 2026 [PMID 42611082]).
  - Home-based exercise (exCR) modestly improves CRF and HRQoL (Winnige et al., 2026 [PMID 42811812]).
- **Emerging**:
  - Mucosal impedance (MI-H/MI-L) may aid staging/ulceration assessment in early gastric cancer (Miyashiro et al., 2027 [PMID 42781318]).
  - Sizi Powder + TDP therapy shows promise for lymphedema post-breast cancer (Zeng et al., 2026 [PMID 42826302]).

**Drugs**
**Evidence-supported but limited**
  - **Trastuzumab deruxtecan + pertuzumab**: PFS benefit in HER2+ metastatic breast cancer (Tolaney et al., 2026 [PMID 41160818]).
  - **Abemaciclib + fulvestrant**: PFS improvement in advanced breast cancer post-CDK4/6 inhibitor (Kalinsky et al., 2025 [PMID 39693591]).
- **Emerging**: Graphene-based materials explored for perioperative cancer management (Bai et al., 2026 [PMID 42829697]).
