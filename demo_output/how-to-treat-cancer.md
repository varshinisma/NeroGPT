# How to treat cancer?

**Evidence searched on 2026-10-09** (PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA drug labels, web)

## Clinical summary

**Bottom line**
The retrieved evidence supports **multidisciplinary supportive care approaches** for cancer patients, including **psychosocial interventions** (e.g., cognitive therapies for neurocognitive outcomes), **symptom management tools** (e.g., mHealth apps for advanced cancer), **behavioral therapies** (e.g., speech therapy for postoperative pain and function), and **complementary therapies** (e.g., external applications like *Sizi Powder* for lymphedema). These strategies address **symptom relief, functional recovery, and quality-of-life outcomes**, but **no direct evidence on systemic cancer treatment (e.g., chemotherapy, immunotherapy, or targeted therapies) was retrieved**.

**Most important recent finding**

**Immediate context**
The evidence **does not address core cancer treatments** (e.g., chemotherapy, radiation, or molecularly targeted therapies). Instead, it focuses on **supportive and symptomatic interventions** (e.g., pain management, lymphedema reduction, psychosocial support) during or after cancer care. No direct evidence on **curative or disease-modifying treatments** was found.

## Latest findings
- **2026-10-01**: Retrospective cohort study, **Sizi Powder external application combined with TDP therapy** for upper limb lymphedema after breast cancer surgery showed a potential benefit in reducing swelling and improving functional outcomes [8].
- **2026-09-29**: Review article, **Graphene-based materials (GBMs)** were proposed as a multifunctional platform for perioperative cancer management, though no direct efficacy data were provided [9].
- **2026-09-29**: Meta-analysis, **Home-based exercise interventions incorporating cardiac rehabilitation principles** improved cardiorespiratory fitness (CRF) and health-related quality of life (HRQoL) in cancer patients [7].
- **2026-09-01**: Randomized controlled trial, **Behavioral speech therapy (BST)** reduced postoperative pain and improved physical function in lung cancer patients undergoing thoracoscopic surgery [5].

## Key studies

1. **Kalinsky et al. (2025)**, Phase III randomized controlled trial: Abemaciclib + fulvestrant extended progression-free survival (HR 0.73) vs. placebo + fulvestrant in advanced breast cancer [2].
2. **Qiu et al. (2024)**, Phase III randomized controlled trial: Tislelizumab + chemotherapy improved overall survival vs. placebo + chemotherapy in advanced gastric cancer [3].
3. **Zhang et al. (2026)**, Randomized controlled trial: Behavioral speech therapy (BST) reduced postoperative pain and improved physical function in lung cancer patients [5].
4. **Paterson et al. (2026)**, Systematic review/meta-analysis: Cognitive interventions improved neurocognitive outcomes (SMD 0.51) in pediatric/adolescent cancer patients [4].

## Conflicting evidence
None was retrieved.

## What remains under investigation
- **Efficacy**: Neoadjuvant inhaled azacytidine + platinum-based chemotherapy + durvalumab for early-stage NSCLC (Phase 1/2 suspended pending modifications; NCT06694454).
- **Effectiveness**: Long-term outcomes of home-based cardiac rehab interventions in diverse cancer populations (adherence and safety data remain inconsistent).
- **Feasibility**: Multidisciplinary supportive care protocols for rare, severe toxicities (e.g., taxane-induced nail toxicity) in real-world settings.
- **Association**: Exploratory analyses of graphene-based materials for perioperative cancer management (no direct efficacy data yet; [9]).
- **Effectiveness**: Comparative effectiveness of Sizi Powder + TDP therapy vs. standard lymphedema management (requires prospective validation).
