# What are the treatment for the cancer?

**Evidence searched on 2026-10-09** (PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA drug labels, web)

## Clinical summary

**Bottom line**
The retrieved evidence supports **multidisciplinary supportive and symptomatic treatments** for cancer patients, including psychosocial interventions (e.g., cognitive therapies for neurocognitive outcomes), behavioral speech therapy for postoperative pain and function, and complementary therapies (e.g., Sizi Powder + TDP for lymphedema). **Systemic oncologic therapies (e.g., chemotherapy, targeted drugs) and surgical interventions are not addressed in the retrieved data**, though FDA-approved anticancer drugs exist for cancer treatment.

**Most important recent finding**
Behavioral speech therapy (BST) reduced postoperative pain and improved physical function in lung cancer patients at 1 and 3 months (MD −0.63 to −0.88, *P* < 0.05). [1]

**Immediate context**
The evidence focuses on **supportive and symptomatic management** (e.g., pain, lymphedema, psychosocial support) rather than systemic cancer therapies. **No direct evidence on chemotherapy, immunotherapy, or targeted drugs** was retrieved.

## Latest findings
- **Advance publication (issue dated 2027 Apr)**: **Case series** – Gel immersion endoscopic mucosal resection (GI-EMR) achieved an 85% en bloc resection rate for superficial esophageal squamous neoplasms, though one procedure was discontinued due to post-chemoradiotherapy scarring [2].
- **Advance publication (issue dated 2027 Apr)**: **Feasibility series** – Rectal flushing-assisted saline immersion endoscopic submucosal dissection (ESD) demonstrated technical feasibility but did not establish efficacy or safety; hemostatic measures were descriptive [3].
- **2026 Sep**: **Randomized controlled trial** – Behavioral speech therapy (BST) reduced postoperative pain and improved physical function in lung cancer patients after thoracoscopic surgery at 1 and 3 months [1].
- **2026 Oct**: **Retrospective cohort study** – Sizi Powder external application combined with TDP therapy showed potential benefits for upper limb lymphedema after breast cancer surgery, though larger prospective studies are needed [4].

## Key studies

1. **Sehn et al. (2026)**, **Randomized controlled trial**: Tafasitamab + lenalidomide + rituximab significantly lowered progression risk in relapsed/refractory follicular lymphoma [5].
2. **Qiu et al. (2024)**, **Randomized controlled trial**: Tislelizumab + chemotherapy improved overall survival in advanced gastric cancer vs. placebo + chemo [7].
3. **Tilly et al. (2022)**, **Randomized controlled trial**: Polatuzumab vedotin + R-CHOP improved progression-free survival in untreated diffuse large B-cell lymphoma [10].
4. **Uda et al. (2027)**, **Case series**: Gel immersion EMR achieved 85% en bloc resection for superficial esophageal squamous neoplasms, though limited by scarring [2].

## Conflicting evidence
None was retrieved.

## What remains under investigation
- **Neoadjuvant inhaled azacytidine + platinum-based chemo + durvalumab** (NCT06694454, **Phase 1/2 suspended**) – Exploring efficacy of epigenetic-immunotherapy in early-stage NSCLC; suspended pending protocol modifications.
- **Predictors of relapse in ovarian/fallopian tube cancer** (NCT00086567, **Completed**) – Investigating serum proteomic signatures for relapse prediction; results not yet posted.
- **Behavioral speech therapy (BST)** – Feasibility and efficacy for postoperative pain management in lung cancer patients; requires larger prospective validation.
- **Sizi Powder + TDP therapy** – Retrospective evidence suggests benefits for upper limb lymphedema post-breast cancer surgery; prospective trials needed.
- **Graphene-based materials** – Experimental platform for perioperative cancer management; no clinical efficacy data yet.
